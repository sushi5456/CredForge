CredForge is an **AI-powered lending and crowdfunding risk-intelligence platform** developed for **Smart India Hackathon 2026** (Problem Statement ID: **SIH199**). It addresses information asymmetry and default risks in tertiary sectors (Hospitality, Financial Services, Entertainment, and Retail) by transforming raw business data into continuous, explainable credit intelligence.

Rather than relying on one-time background checks, CredForge monitors business health continuously—enabling transparent, data-driven decisions for lenders, borrowers, and platform administrators.

---

## 📌 Problem & Solution

### The Problem
* **Limited Access & Complex Processes:** Small and medium businesses struggle to secure capital through traditional channels due to lengthy evaluations and rigid criteria.
* **High Default Risk & Lack of Visibility:** Lenders face information asymmetry, insufficient business data, and a lack of real-time monitoring post-funding.
* **Static Verification:** Traditional credit scoring relies on historical, one-off checks that fail to catch early business deterioration.

### The CredForge Solution
CredForge bridges the gap between verified businesses and impact-driven capital by implementing a **Continuous Intelligence Pipeline**:
1. **Multi-Source Data Ingestion:** Validates identities and financial records across GST, MCA, UDYAM, and banking data.
2. **AI Business Trust Health Score:** Uses machine learning models to assess creditworthiness and detect financial anomalies.
3. **Explainable AI (XAI):** Employs SHAP and LIME algorithms to provide transparent, human-readable scoring breakdowns.
4. **Continuous Post-Funding Monitoring:** Monitors ongoing transaction health and delivers early-warning risk alerts to investors.

## ✨ Feature Breakdown

### 💼 Lenders & Investors
* **Opportunity Discovery:** Browse verified business campaigns filtered by your specific risk-tolerance levels.
* **Risk Intelligence Dashboard:** Access granular financial health scores, default estimations, and explainable risk factors before committing capital.
* **Portfolio & Health Alerts:** Track active investments with real-time alerts whenever a business's health metrics shift.

### 🏢 Borrowers & Businesses
* **Simplified Onboarding:** Apply for business funding through an automated identity and document validation workflow.
* **Campaign Management:** Real-time visibility into funding progress, escrow tracking, and digital repayment schedules (e-NACH).
* **Actionable Growth Insights:** Receive AI-generated suggestions to improve operational metrics and enhance future funding readiness[cite: 1].

### 🛡️ Admin & Compliance
* **Verification Workflows:** Review automated cross-checks from official registries (GSTN, MCA, UDYAM, Aadhaar/KYC)[cite: 1].
* **Platform Security & Fraud Detection:** Detect fake documents, unusual activity patterns, and operational risks automatically[cite: 1].
* **Audit Trails & System Analytics:** Oversee compliance logs, manage campaign listings, and maintain system integrity[cite: 1].
